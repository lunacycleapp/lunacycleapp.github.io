Luna — Cycle Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2025 Last updated: 1 August 2025 Version: 1.0
Luna is a menstrual cycle tracking app. Your health data is personal and sensitive. This policy explains exactly what we collect, where it lives, who can see it, and how you can delete it — in plain language.
01

Data we collect

Luna collects only what is necessary to provide its features.

Category Examples Required?
Cycle data Period start/end dates, cycle length, predicted next period Yes — core feature
Symptoms & mood Pain level, flow intensity, mood, energy, notes you write Optional
Profile Display name, avatar, goal (track / pregnant / menopause) Optional
Google account info Name, email, profile photo — only when you sign in with Google Optional
Settings App language, PIN/biometric lock preference, partner sharing toggles Yes — app function
Partner-shared data Cycle phase, next period date, day of cycle — only fields you enable Optional — partner mode only

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone, or camera. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics trackers.

02

How and where data is stored

On your device (primary storage)

All cycle logs, symptoms, moods, and notes are stored in an encrypted local database on your device. They do not leave your phone unless you explicitly enable a backup or partner feature.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you sign in with Google and choose to back up, Luna writes a single file (luna_backup.json) to your Google Drive's private appDataFolder. This folder is only accessible to Luna — not visible in your Drive file browser and not accessible to other apps or to us.

Firebase / Firestore (partner mode only)

When partner mode is active, a limited snapshot of your data is published to Google Firebase Firestore under a randomly-generated partner code you control. No name or account identifier is attached to this data. Your Firebase authentication is anonymous — we never store your Google email in Firebase.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for any commercial purpose.

03

Google services

Luna integrates with two Google services, both optional:

  • Google Sign-In — used to link your identity so Luna can write to your Drive's private appDataFolder. We request only the drive.appdata scope.
  • Google Drive — used solely for the backup feature described above. We read and write only the file Luna itself created.

Google's own privacy policy governs how Google processes data: policies.google.com/privacy

You can revoke Luna's access from your Google Account security settings, or by using Settings → Complete wipe inside the app.

04

Partner mode

Partner mode lets a trusted person view a limited, curated view of your cycle on their own device. You stay in full control of what is shared.

  • You generate a private partner code and share it only with the person you choose.
  • You individually toggle each piece of data: cycle phase, next period date, day of cycle, and fertility window.
  • The data published under your partner code is not linked to your name, email, or Google account in our systems.
  • You can revoke partner access at any time from Settings, which deletes the Firestore record.

The partner's device only reads — it cannot modify your data.

05

AI assistant

Luna includes an optional AI chat assistant. Messages you send are processed by a third-party AI API. Before using it, you provide an API key — stored locally on your device only, never transmitted to Luna's servers.

The content of your conversations is governed by the privacy policy of the AI provider you configure. No chat messages are stored in Firebase or Google Drive.

06

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — all data is visible within the app at all times.
  • Delete your data — use Settings → Emergency Wipe → Local device to erase local data, or Complete wipe to also delete your Firebase account and Google Drive backup. Both require PIN or biometric confirmation.
  • Export your data — your Google Drive backup (luna_backup.json) is a readable JSON file you can download from your Google Drive at any time.
  • Correct your data — all entries can be edited or deleted from within the app.
  • Object to processing — you may disable optional features (backup, partner mode, AI) at any time from Settings.

If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

07

Security

Luna uses Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences and an encrypted local database to protect data at rest on your device. The optional PIN lock and biometric lock add a second layer of access control.

Data in transit to Firebase and Google Drive is encrypted by HTTPS/TLS. No security measure is absolute. We recommend enabling device encryption and a PIN lock for the strongest protection of your health data.

08

Children's privacy

Luna is not directed at children under 13 years of age and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

09

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and — for material changes — notify you through an in-app notice. Your continued use of Luna after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
